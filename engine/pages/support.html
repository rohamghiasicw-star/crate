Support

Addify finds the exact version of the song in a clip. Slowed, sped up, remix, or the original. Here's how to use it, and how to get hold of us.

How it works

Share a clip and Addify names the song and reads its true speed in a few seconds. Then it hunts for the exact upload that matches the audio, and only crowns one when the audio really lines up.

Using it

Share a link

Copy a TikTok or Instagram link and paste it on the home screen, or share the clip straight into Addify from your phone's share sheet. Public clips only.

Listen

Tap the microphone and Addify listens for 12 seconds. It names the song and tells you if it's slowed or sped up. The recording is deleted as soon as the lookup ends.

Save it

Every result links out to the song on the platform where it lives. Connect Spotify and matches can go straight into a "Found with Addify" playlist.

Your data

Delete everything

Go to Profile › Delete account. That erases any feedback you sent from our server, clears everything the app stored on your device, and reloads. No email, no call, no waiting.

If you cleared your browser data first and want feedback rows erased anyway, email [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] with the link you scanned and roughly when.

Disconnect Spotify

Profile › Sign out of Spotify, or open Settings and tap Disconnect. Your Spotify tokens only ever live on your device, so signing out removes them completely. To revoke Addify on Spotify's side too, remove it under the apps section of your Spotify account settings.

Results

Why it says "closest match" instead of naming the exact version

Addify only crowns an upload when the audio genuinely matches. If nothing clears that bar, it shows you the closest thing it found and says so. That's on purpose. A confident wrong answer is worse than an honest "not sure".

Known limits

  • Long Instagram share links (the ones with ?igsh=) resolve as private. Open the reel and copy the plain link instead.
  • On a mashup, the multi-song pass only reads the first 12 seconds, so later songs in a long clip can come back wrong.
  • A clip with no lead at all, no fingerprint, no caption, no comments, is a genuine dead end.
  • Private clips can't be scanned.
The iOS app

The native iOS app isn't out yet. Today Addify runs as a web app you can add to your home screen.

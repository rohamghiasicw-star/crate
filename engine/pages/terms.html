The service

What Addify is

Addify is an identification service. You give it a clip, it tells you which song and which version is playing. Results link out to the platform where that song lives.

Addify does not play, stream, store or let you download the clips or the songs. Where an official or licensed version exists, that's the link we offer first.

Results are best-effort

Identification is automated and can be wrong. Addify says when it isn't sure instead of guessing, but a confident answer can still be a miss. Check before you rely on it.

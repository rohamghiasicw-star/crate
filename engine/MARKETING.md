## Reposters

Reposters warm up Instagram and TikTok accounts and post exactly what they are told, so we
keep full control of the creative. Konnor can make more videos per day than one account can
carry — posting more than twice a day on a UGC account hurts it — so reposters multiply
volume without burning any single account.

- **~$100/month per account.**
- Start with **5-10 reposters.**
- Expected effect: roughly 10x posting volume.

## Why the product supports this

The demo IS the pitch, so the app has to survive being filmed:

- The scan screen is the hero shot — dial, percentage, the matched-slice timeline. It has
  to look alive for the seconds the camera is on it.
- "Add to playlist" must be one obvious tap, on camera, with no login detour mid-video.
- **SoundCloud is the differentiator Konnor is selling** (see below) — a bass boosted or
  slowed edit does not exist on Spotify, so an app that saves it somewhere real is doing
  something Shazam structurally cannot.

## Konnor's product argument, worth keeping

> "Our biggest win that Shazam doesn't have is customers being able to connect SoundCloud
> and when they find the exact remix audio they can add it to their SoundCloud playlist.
> Shazam has nothing with SoundCloud. Having that extra app to be able to add to is huge
> especially where most of these reverb songs on edits are available on SoundCloud. Like
> you're not finding a BASS BOOSTED ahhh song to add to Spotify."

And on the results list:

> "'More like this edit' after a match — show other edits of the same song mixed with other
> songs."

Roham's reply is the reason this matters commercially: *"I would still pay to use that even
if it's not the exact right edit but it shows a bunch of the edits and one of them might be
the right one."*

# Clip forensics: root causes behind Roham's 21 wrong grades

Produced 2026-09-05 by 85 agents (3 lenses + 1 judge per clip, then a clustering pass).
Every mechanism below was measured by an agent that downloaded and compared audio itself.
Shazam was off-limits to all of them, so none of these numbers are throttle artifacts.

Engine on a clean sequential run of the 33 graded clips: song named 33/33, edit crowned 12/33.

## Root causes, by clips explained

### dl_priority_starves_plain_original  (4 clips: [3, 5, 14, 24])
- **effort** small   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `_dl_priority` (crate_engine.py:4449) puts `-edit_titled` at tuple index 4 and `-plays` last at index 7, where `edit_titled = bool(EDIT_WORDS.search(t)) and not OTHER_RENDITION.search(t)` (4453), so EVERY non-edit-titled upload sorts below EVERY edit-titled one regardless of play count, and `find_edit`'s `max_dl=14` (4103, applied at `wave1 = _sc_quota(sorted(cands, key=_dl_priority), max_dl)[:max_dl]` on 4499 and re-used for the wave-2 head at 4606) truncates there. Reproduced pool ranks: EsDeeKid's own 3.72M-play master at 29/460 behind 28 edit-titled rows; 'Rae Sremmurd - No Type (Official Video)' at 938M plays at rank 52/558 with ranks 1-51 all 51/51 edit-titled; Mac Miller's plain rows at 38-41 of 433 with 259/433 edit-titled; 'Katy Perry - Roar' at 4.37 BILLION plays at rank 75/518. In all four the 14 fetched rows were 14/14 edit-titled, so the verifier only ever saw edits and the crown could only be an edit title. The structural half, which makes this a defect rather than a tuning miss: `is_official_original` (4852) requires `not EDIT_WORDS.search(c["title"])`, which is the SAME predicate `edit_titled` negates, so the rows rank_key's plain-original tier at 5216 is built to prefer on an unedited clip are exactly the rows the download queue guarantees will not exist - that tier has probably never fired on a popular track, because a viral song always has more than 14 edit-titled uploads. `_dl_priority` also has no uploader-identity leg at all: its `-_artist_hit` leg is a substring test over title+uploader that passed 43 of 164 relevant rows on clip 3 while only 2 rows had the artist as the actual uploader. Downstream casualties measured: on clip 5 the all-edits pool also poisons `ref_paths` (server.py:1692-1697), forcing the `len(refs) < 2` fallback and producing a fabricated 'sped up ~1.30x' on a clip that measures 1.0000x; on clip 3 `official_tilts` (4897) is empty so `bassy_baseline` falls back to the raw clip tilt and the bass badge missed by 0.40 dB. Also the first leg of clip 9.
- **fix** APPEND a plain-original lane to the wave-1 head rather than reordering into it - follow the `_creator_extra` pattern at 3085-3108 where the caller raises max_dl, since the three existing quotas at 3977-4011 displace and would cost some other clip a slot. Reserve slots for the highest-play rows with `title_hits >= 1` and NOT `edit_titled`, fired only when the head contains none. Three corrections to what the per-clip analyses proposed, each forced by comparing them against each other: the lane must be UNCONDITIONAL rather than gated on `edit_label == 'as posted'`, because phase 1 labelled clip 5 'slowed ~0.83x' on audio that measures 1.0000x so that gate would not have fired where it was most needed - safety comes instead from rank_key's `_clip_is_edit` tier at 5216, which already inverts correctly, and from the fact that this changes what gets DOWNLOADED, not what wins; reserve 3-4 slots rather than 2, because on clip 14 the top two plain rows by plays were a truncated 11.5s download and a SoundCloud 403 while the row that verifies at core 1.000 was third; and it must land after the dl_clip truncation fix or the reserved slots fill with short audio. Do NOT simply demote `-edit_titled` - that tier is load-bearing for niche edits and is what the function's own docstring at 4381-4390 was written to protect.
- **risk** Medium. It costs 1-2 of 14 download slots on every clip, so the scored head changes corpus-wide even though the ranking code is untouched. It cannot suppress an edit or veto anything - verify() and rank_key still decide, so 'bass and speed NUDGE, never REJECT' holds. The specific regression to watch is a niche-edit clip where the displaced slots held the real answer; on the four measured clips the displaced rows were an 840-play and a 349-play remix, neither a plausible crown. Five-clip regression gate, run twice, not while the owner is demoing. Two of the four clips (5 and 24) need a second fix as well and will still be wrong after this lands alone.

### decode_window_first_20s  (3 clips: [4, 13, 20])
- **effort** large   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `dl_clip(url, dst, seconds=20)` (crate_engine.py:3806, enforced at 3847 by `--download-sections *0-%d`) fetches only the head of every candidate, and `verify()` re-caps independently at `_decode(cand_path, seconds=20)` (verify.py:433/87) and aligns only within what it is handed, so a long upload whose matching section sits later reads as a near-miss and loses to a shorter upload at the wrong speed or EQ. Measured swings on the correct answer: clip 4's pdl56Px3a_U reads core 0.633 at t=0 and 1.000 at t=20/30/50 with speed 1.0000 at every offset; clip 20's owner-named upload reads 0.8559 on its head and 1.000 with arr 0.8691 at t=115-125 of a 158.6s file (the TikTok sound loops, so the clip's head is the track's tail); clip 24's official master reads 0.7163 at t=0 and 1.000 at t=30/40/50/90-130; clip 9's instrumental reads 0.4433 on the head against 1.000 at 13 of 23 windows; clip 10's correct upload reads 0.372 and 1.000 at t=50-100. The error crosses tier boundaries, which is why it decides crowns: rank_key's `strong_core` boolean (CORE_EDIT 0.62) at ~5150 and its core band (CORE_SAME 0.95 / CORE_MERIT 0.38) at 5188 both sit ABOVE `speed_exact` at 5190, so a head-only misread costs the right upload a rung before its perfect speed lock is ever consulted. Clip 13 is the same defect on the REFERENCE side, which #10 does not describe: `server.py:1707-1712` fetches speed references with the same 20s default and `speed_from_master.confirm_ref` then decodes a 25s head slice, and on that track the intro is the ONLY window in 252s that does not lock - 9 of 12 later windows confirm at mean confidence 0.527-0.635 and read 0.9016-0.9066, so `measured` stayed None and `_crown_tempo_mismatch`'s corroboration escape hatch (server.py:1408-1411) went unevaluated. The rescue that exists for exactly this is unreachable: `dl_seconds = 180` is set only inside `creator_search` (3078-3082) and the `_slide` block at 4716-4756 is gated on creator_upload/creator_link plus core < CORE_KEEP plus spectral >= 0.85, all three of which excluded the correct row on clip 20.
- **fix** Ticket #10's own prescription, in four parts. (a) Candidate side: for candidates longer than ~90s whose head core lands in the ambiguous 0.35-0.75 band or whose title carries edit/loop/extended words, fetch a strided set of 25s windows (t=0,30,60,90...), keep the best core, and record the offset in the existing `aligned_at` field which is null on every row of every payload today. The engine's own 15s grid `range(15, dur-20, 15)` was measured to land on clip 20's correct offset. (b) Duration-gate rather than creator-gate: move `dl_seconds = 180` out of `creator_search` and widen `_align`'s admission at 4716 to `_dur_s(c) > 45`, raising its ceiling from CORE_KEEP 0.50 to CORE_SAME 0.95 and lowering its spectral evidence floor from 0.85 to about 0.72 - both were measured as blocking clip 20's correct row at 0.848 core and 0.7833 spectral. (c) Reference side, new: fetch the ~5 speed references at seconds=90 and have `confirm_ref` / `measure_consensus` evaluate offsets 0/25/60 and keep the best-locking slice instead of always reading the head. Measured cost on the same URL was 1.3s versus 1.8s. (d) It depends on the dl_clip truncation fix or a 180s request comes back as 125s and the clip's section is clipped off. Do NOT reintroduce the blind 90s re-decode - it was removed for making near-misses worse.
- **risk** High, and it is why this is not first despite being the biggest payoff. It changes core on every long candidate in the corpus, which moves ranking everywhere, and on clip 24 the naive version makes things WORSE in isolation by lifting the wrong bass-boosted crown from 0.686 to a provably-same-audio 1.000 where it wins the core band outright. It also makes `measured` available on clips that have none, which feeds two crown gates. Do NOT lower `confirm_ref`'s conf_min as a shortcut: on clip 13 that admits a 1.198x off-speed edit and produces a confident 'sped up ~1.20x', the exact opposite of the truth. Adversarial control that must be kept: sliding produced zero false confirmations across 11 windows of an unrelated song (max mean conf 0.379 against the true master's 0.527-0.635). Five-clip gate twice, and re-check clips 20 and 30, the other vetoes that fired with no measurement.

### short_clip_speed_floor  (2 clips: [26, 30])
- **effort** small   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `speed_from_master._win_speeds` (speed_from_master.py:119) hardcodes `W=12.0, step=4.0` and walks the CLIP under `while t + 6.0 <= dur`, so the number of windows it can EVER emit is floor((dur-6)/4)+1. `confirm_ref` (line 148) opens with `if len(ws) < min_win: return False` and min_win defaults to 3. Three windows requires dur >= 14.0s exactly. Below that, `confirm_ref` returns False for every possible reference regardless of match quality, so both arms of the reference block at server.py:1689-1729 come back empty, `measure_consensus` is never called, `measured` stays None, and `res["speed"]` keeps the phase-1 Shazam counter-sweep placeholder. I verified the loop, the defaults and the arithmetic directly in the source. Clip 26 decodes at 6.990s (one window) and clip 30 at 10.799s (two). On clip 26 the engine had already downloaded four clean masters and one produced two windows agreeing to 0.1% at conf 0.56 and 0.48 - refused on COUNT, not quality; bypassing the gate on those same files returns `{speed: 0.9327, label: 'slowed ~0.93x', confident: true, agree: 4/4, spread: 0.002}`, which is the owner's verdict from the engine's own code. The corpus proves the boundary rather than merely correlating with it: of 33 payloads, all 9 with clip_secs < 14.0 carry no speed_measured / speed_confirmed / speed_refs, and all 16 that carry a measurement are >= 14.2s. Zero exceptions, perfect separation at exactly the constant. A controlled truncation ladder on a synthetic same-recording pair puts the cliff at 14.0s to the tenth of a second.
- **fix** One function, speed_from_master.py:119. Derive the stride from the clip instead of hardcoding it: compute dur first and, when floor((dur-6)/step)+1 < min_win, drop to W=8.0/step=2.0 (and W=6.5/step=1.5 under ~9s). Bit-for-bit no-op for every clip >= 14s, so no long clip's behaviour moves. Measured on clip 30: the right reference goes from 2 windows to 3 tight ones at 0.5751/0.5754/0.5760, confirm_ref returns True with min_win untouched, and the label ships. Prefer this over lowering `confirm_ref`'s min_win to 2: both fix these two clips identically, but lowering min_win weakens the same-recording guard on every long clip where it currently works, while the stride change only touches clips presently guaranteed to fail. Fix it in `_win_speeds` rather than in `confirm_ref` because `measure_consensus` is NOT independently short-clip-safe either - on a single perfectly aligned core-1.000 reference it returns confident=false, 'insufficient reference windows', since it needs len(logs) >= 2; one stride change repairs both call sites.
- **risk** Medium, and specifically bounded. It newly produces a `measured` value on 9 of the 33 graded clips, and `measured` is read by both `_crown_tempo_mismatch` and `_crown_contradicts`, so crown outcomes change on clips that today get no measurement at all. The named hazard is the SIM-B simulation recorded at server.py:1730-1740, which regressed n=24, n=29 and n=30 when speed_confirmed was wired into the gates - all three are sub-14s clips, so its inputs change under this patch and its old conclusion cannot be assumed; re-run it. On clip 26 specifically, removing the refusal lets a core-0.625 row survive to the crown on a clip where nothing in the pool is actually the source, so verify CORE_KEEP and `unsure` still catch it - the label fix must not buy a wrong-version crown. Five-clip gate twice.

### ungated_multi_song_emission  (2 clips: [7, 18])
- **effort** small   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `_fingerprint_core` (crate_engine.py:2440-2448, which I read directly) de-duplicates the phase-1 scan hits on `_title_key` alone and then sets `primary = dict(merged[0])`, `primary["songs"] = merged`, `primary["multi"] = len(merged) > 1` with NO support requirement whatsoever, so ONE window out of six is enough to create a second song and the EARLIEST window by `at` becomes the base song. The tier-2 mashup pass does its job: MASHUP_MIN_SUPPORT = 2 (2495) rejects the pair, which is why neither payload carries a `mashup` or `sections` key. But the reject branch (2648-2653) writes `fp["mashup_rejected"]` and returns WITHOUT touching `fp["songs"]` or `fp["multi"]`, and `mashup_rejected` / `mashup_probes` appear zero times in server.py and crate.html - they are write-only. server.py:822 then emits `res["songs"]` on `fp.get("multi")` alone, never on `fp.get("mashup")`, and crate.html:1576 renders the block on `secs.length>1 || alt.length>1`, printing 'N songs in this clip'. So a one-window Shazam mis-ID that the engine's own floor ALREADY refused reaches the screen as an answer, never downloaded and never verified. Clip 7 was reproduced in process with Shazam stubbed out: feeding the two recorded hits through those three lines yields exactly the shipped two-song list, and eq-invariant frame cosine on the six scan windows fits one continuous timeline (slope 1.00131, max residual 0.036s against a 0.0464s frame) with the phantom's window matching the true master at 0.4127 and the phantom upload at only 0.3123. Clip 18 is the same three lines biting the OTHER half: the earliest window carried a cover-remix catalogue row, so `merged[0]` made it the base, and `res["base_song"]` is written once at server.py:815-818 with no crown ever rewriting it - the named song reads core 0.3708 across 41 sliding windows, below what an unrelated track reaches by chance (0.4071), while the real song reads 0.8242.
- **fix** server.py:822, `if fp.get("multi"):` -> `if fp.get("mashup") and fp.get("multi"):`, so `res["songs"]` ships only under the same condition that already gates `res["mashup"]` and `res["sections"]` two lines below - i.e. only when tier 2 proved the second song against the audio. Gate at the emission point rather than pruning inside the reject branch, because `annotate_mashup` is wrapped in `except Exception: pass` inside `fingerprint()` and an exception would skip a prune while server.py would still see multi=True. Pair it with crate.html:1576 dropping the `|| alt.length>1` fallback so the block renders off `sections` only. Do NOT fix this by name-matching (suffix-merging `_key_alias`, adding terms to `_MILL`): the phantom on clip 7 was measured as a genuinely different recording from the master, so the next mis-ID may share no words at all. Clip 18 needs one more step for its hero title, listed separately in the plan.
- **risk** Very low - the lowest on the board. It removes a payload field; it changes no crown, no candidate and no ordering, so the five-clip gate's crowns cannot move. Measured against the whole graded corpus rather than argued: 2 fixed, 0 regressions, with perfect separation - the only other multi-song payloads (n4 and n19) both carry `mashup` and keep their list, and n17 (the genuine missed mashup) has one song and is untouched. The only honest caveat is that clip 18's graded verdict is satisfied while its hero title still reads the wrong song until the base-arbitration step lands.

### crown_refusal_no_fallthrough  (2 clips: [4, 20])
- **effort** small   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `top = verified[0]` at server.py:1647, and when `_crown_tempo_mismatch` / `_crown_contradicts` / `_time_reversed_null` refuse that single row, `top` is set to None with no walk down the rest of `verified`, and server.py:1790 then hardcodes `exact = candidates[0]`. So one refusal at index 0 discards a whole shelf whose next row may be gate-clean, and the UI (crate.html:1660-1712) prints 'Held back on purpose' and lists the candidates in payload order, never re-sorted, each row an anchor to the upload with a play glyph. On clip 4 the refusal was arithmetically correct (joana's upload, |log2 0.8833| = 0.179 against _TEMPO_TOL 0.06) but the row at index 1 was the answer: pdl56Px3a_U at core 0.632, over the CORE_KEEP 0.50 floor, vspeed 1.0004, measured at 0.8197x absolute against the clip's 0.8203x - a 0.07% match - while the row the owner actually clicked plays 13.4% FASTER than his clip. On clip 20 the same pattern: `_crown_tempo_mismatch` correctly refused the YouTube row for a 20% gap and `_crown_contradicts` refused a second row for titling itself a speed edit, while the owner's own named URL sat at index 1 and was measured as the ONLY row of the six that clears every crown gate (tempo None, contradicts None, no _SPEED_CLAIM match). This is an amplifier rather than a root - on both clips the underlying cause is the decode window mis-ordering the shelf - but it is what turned a mis-order into no answer at all, and on clip 20 it is what stood between the owner and the exact link he named in his verdict.
- **fix** server.py:1786-1790. When a gate nulls `top`, walk down `verified` for the next row that still clears CORE_KEEP and passes the SAME three gates, and set `exact` to that survivor instead of hardcoding `candidates[0]`. About five lines, no new download and no new verify. On clip 4 that crowns pdl56Px3a_U; on clip 20 it crowns soundcloud.com/k6edxqtrlpcq/esdeekid-mist-speeds-reverb, the owner's own URL, verbatim.
- **risk** Low, with one hard requirement. It provably cannot regress a currently-succeeding crown because it only executes on a path where `top` is already None. But it converts 'no crown' into 'crown', which lives next door to the 'never crown below the score floor' doctrine, so the fall-through must re-apply CORE_KEEP and all three gates to the survivor rather than bypassing them - on the two measured clips the survivors are 0.632 and 0.848, both clear. Honest framing: on clip 20 this delivers the right link while every number on the shelf is still measured on the wrong 20 seconds, so it is the cheapest patch and not the cure.

### false_creator_provenance  (1 clips: [8])
- **effort** small   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `cred_toks` is built by tokenising the TikTok credit DISPLAY NAME (crate_engine.py:4426-4430) with only a 4-character minimum: 'original sound - Jack-Dean' yields ['jack','dean','hanbury_2011'] and 'original sound - SETH' yields ['seth','sethpetermusic']. `_creator_source` (4991, match at 5007, which I read directly) then searches those tokens across title AND uploader, so the common first name 'dean' hits 'Bobby Welcomes Dean to Heaven | Supernatural' - a TV character with no relation to the sound owner - and 'seth' hits soundcloud.com/drekingston, an unrelated account whose display name is 'Seth D.'. Its only other conditions are same=True and core >= CORE_EDIT 0.62, which speech-over-music audio satisfies. `_creator_source` is rank_key element [1], above `_artist_own` [4], `strong_core` [5], the CORE_SAME/CORE_MERIT band [6] and `speed_exact` [7], so on clip 8 both surviving rows tied at [0] and the crown was DECIDED at [1] - no audio-merit tier was ever consulted between a Supernatural rip and Bryan Adams' own master. The same tokens also drive `creator_hit` at `_dl_priority` index 3, which is what pinned a 645-play 2014 upload at download rank 1 of 558 on clip 5. Two corroborations nobody used: clip 5's payload carries sound_created 1700204059 (2023-11-17) against a 2014-10-27 upload date, making the provenance claim impossible on its face; and a validated rank_key reimplementation that reproduces clip 8's shipped ordering exactly shows the counterfactuals cleanly - fixing the window alone still crowns Supernatural, fixing the collision alone crowns a 1.20x remix, fixing both crowns 'Bryan Adams - Heaven (Classic Version)' at core 1.000, slowed 0.9002x, which is the owner's verdict.
- **fix** crate_engine.py:5007 - `_creator_source` must stop matching cred_toks against the candidate TITLE and match the UPLOADER field only, exactly as `_dl_priority`'s own `creator_hit` already does at 4451-4452; keep the title path only for the `creator_link` branch at 5000-5003, where provenance is stated rather than inferred. Reinforce at 4426-4430 by not splitting a personal display name into standalone tokens - require the resolved @handle or tokens of length >= 6, since the len>=4 filter does not stop a common first name. Both `creator_hit` (download rank) and `_creator_source` (rank_key index 1) read the same list, so one tightening fixes both stages together.
- **risk** Medium. It demotes rows that today win at rank_key index [1], above every audio tier, so crowns move on any clip carrying an 'original sound - X' credit - which is most of them. The legitimate case it must not break is the genuine editor's own upload, which is why the `creator_link` branch (a file the creator linked in their own comments) must keep its title path. Note this fix alone does not deliver clip 8: it crowns a 1.20x remix until the decode window lands. It does complete clip 5 once the plain-original lane is in. Five-clip gate twice.

### reversed_null_control_misfires  (1 clips: [12])
- **effort** small   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `_time_reversed_null` (server.py:1279, called at 1782, decision at 1321 `if rc >= fwd_core`) destroyed the correct crown. candidates[0] was 'Chief Keef - I Dont Like Bass Boosted' at core 0.961, vspeed 0.9997 and bass 0.2 dB off the clip - it cleared every score and tempo gate, and the last gate refused it, so `exact` went null and the UI fell back to a shelf whose rows 3-5 are the Kanye/Big Sean remix the owner objected to. The deeper fault is in `_arr_score` (verify.py:291): it returns `max(mean_cos)` over ~496 admissible lags times 5 frequency shifts, and verify.py:499 then takes `max(ungated, gated)` on top, and it never tests whether that maximum is a PEAK. A true match produces a genuine alignment spike; a reversed or unrelated recording produces a flat plateau whose top argmax still reports as if it were an alignment, and the two get compared as the same statistic. Measured lag distributions: the correct forward match is the only pair with a peak, max 0.3944 standing 0.0620 above its own 99th percentile, while every reversed copy and every wrong candidate has a prominence of 0.0058-0.0081, about ten times smaller. The decisive control, which is the null-of-the-null: Adele 'Hello' - a recording the engine never considered - reversed against this clip reaches raw arr 0.4357, ABOVE the correct crown's genuine forward peak of 0.3944, and reversed core 0.9591 against the crown's 1.000. Reversal RAISES arr on 4 of the 5 pool candidates regardless of whether they are the right recording, converging on 0.40-0.46, so the reversed number is a property of the clip, not of the pair. Core saturation is the amplifier: fp 0.5322 sits below FP_LO 0.55 so the fingerprint path normalises to exactly 0.0 and core rides entirely on arr, which confines the gate's whole entry window to arr in [0.4325, 0.4500] - 0.0175 wide.
- **fix** One constant at the entry guard, server.py:1310: change `fwd_core < E.CORE_SAME` (0.95) to `< 0.999`. The function's own docstring says it exists because 'a core of 1.000 is supposed to mean provably the same recording', and all four of its calibration cases (STRUCT, Omens, ur LYING, GALE) have a forward score of exactly 1.000 - it was built to second-guess SATURATED claims, and CORE_SAME also admits unsaturated ones like this clip's 0.961. Raising the entry keeps all four calibration cases eligible, keeps GALE caught, and makes clip 12 ineligible. Do NOT reach for the 'compare raw arr with a margin' variant: it happens to save this clip (+0.028 against a 0.05 margin) but Adele reversed still inverts it, so it is a band-aid on a clip-specific number. The durable follow-up is to have `_arr_score` return peak prominence alongside the max and have every null control compare prominence rather than magnitude - measured separation here is roughly ten to one, and unlike the absolute score it has no ceiling.
- **risk** Low for the constant - it touches nothing that currently passes, since the only rows whose behaviour changes are those with forward core in [0.95, 0.999). Higher for the prominence rework, which changes arr semantics everywhere. One honest caveat that is not an alternative explanation but a reason to distrust the result: a re-measure today read forward core 0.938 where the graded run recorded 0.961, and both sit inside that 0.0175-wide arr band, so whether this clip gets crowned at all turns on roughly 0.005 of arr. Treat the gate as unstable rather than merely wrong, and count this clip as an instance of open ticket #4.

### eq_invariant_leaks_loudness_envelope  (1 clips: [11])
- **effort** small   **confidence** high
- **mechanism** `_eq_invariant` (verify.py:279-288, which I read directly) removes each BAND's time-mean and then unit-L2-normalises each frame, but it never removes each FRAME's level - the docstring claims level invariance and the code only half delivers it. On a clip with a big loudness step, every quiet frame becomes a vector of all-negative band residuals and every loud frame all-positive, so after normalisation the quiet frames collapse onto one unit direction and the loud frames onto its negation, and `_arr_score`'s mean cosine then scores loudness-envelope agreement rather than musical content. This clip is the owner's description exactly: 0-5s at -22.8 to -31.2 dB, a sound-effect spike at 5.25s, music from 6.00s at -7.8 to -12.6 dB, a ~14 dB step. Rank-1 SVD energy share of the whole clip's `_eq_invariant` is 0.665 with mean |cos| to the mean direction 0.776; split into halves those fall to 0.224 and 0.208, so the correlation lives in the step between them. arr is then inflated past ARR_HI 0.45 and `core = max(_norm(fp,0.55,0.72), _norm(arr,0.10,0.45))` (verify.py:543) clamps to exactly 1.000, and crate.html:1360 renders raw `c.core` as 'N% match' on every row independent of the crown gates - so four rows printed '100% match' even though the server had already refused the crown. The falsifying experiment settles causation: 6s of numpy-generated noise followed by 8s of Daft Punk scores core 1.0000 against an unrelated Tove Lo upload, and the same 8s with the lead-in removed scores 0.1398. Unrelated real songs against the real clip: Adele 0.9011, Rihanna 0.7348. Time reversal, which destroys the recording and preserves the envelope, gives forward 1.000 / reversed 1.000 on the crown and actually IMPROVES Adele from 0.9011 to 1.0000.
- **fix** One line in verify.py at 286, double-centring before the L2 norm: add `M = M - M.mean(axis=1, keepdims=True)` after the existing axis=0 centring. Measured on the synthetic control where ground truth is known by construction: unrelated candidates drop to 0.017-0.075 while the true source holds 1.0000 with fp 0.9731, taking the true/false ratio from 1.00 to 13.3. On the real clip no row reads 100% any more (crown 1.000 -> 0.651, Adele 0.901 -> 0.310). The alternative fix was tested and does not work: raising ARR_HI to 0.93 still scores a completely unrelated song at 0.517 against 0.662 for the true source, ratio 1.28 - it hides the '100%' text and leaves the wrong ordering intact.
- **risk** High, and it is the reason this sits low in the plan despite being one line. It changes arr on EVERY clip in the corpus, so every core and every ranking that rides on arr moves. It touches no threshold, tier or gate, so nothing that ranks on `fp` or on the `score` tilt penalty is affected.
